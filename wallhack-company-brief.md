# Company Brief: WALLHACK

## Overview
WALLHACK (wallhack.com) is a European gaming-peripherals brand that designs **performance-focused glass mousepads** and complementary gear for competitive PC gamers. The product philosophy centers on precision, consistency, and muscle memory — surfaces engineered through tribological research to deliver predictable, repeatable glide for split-second aim, wrapped in a minimalist Scandinavian design language.

WALLHACK is headquartered in Denmark and operates as a self-owned company (~11–50 employees). Its core business is **glass gaming mousepads**, supported by cloth pads, mouse skates, sleeves, bundles, and limited-edition collectibles.

## Current Flagship Products
WALLHACK's current lineup is built around its **5th-generation glass surfaces**, which are tuned for different play styles. The three key current products are:

### SP-005 — Speed/balanced glass mousepad
The current flagship of the long-running "SP" surface line. It uses a micro-etched, faceted crystal micro-pattern that reduces the mouse's contact area for a fast, fluid, speed-oriented glide, while a subtle surface texture still provides light tactile feedback. Built from tempered, ultra-durable glass in a slim 2.5mm low-profile form, with CNC-tapered edges to reduce wrist contact and a concave silicone base grid for stability. It's a direct evolution of the SP-004, improved across nearly every aspect. **Price: ~$129** (Black / White).

### CR-005 — Control surface glass mousepad
The control-oriented 5th-gen surface ("Crater" control surface). Its microscopic concave crater-like structures generate high static and kinetic friction for a stable, deliberate glide; as downward pressure increases, resistance rises, giving pressure-responsive stopping power for precise tracking and scoped aim. Same 2.5mm tempered-glass low-profile build, tapered edge, and redesigned concave-grip silicone base (510×440mm). **Price: ~$129** (Black).

### SP-004A — Compact / travel glass mousepad
A smaller, more travel-friendly version of the SP-004 with the same high-accuracy micro-etched glass surface. The silicate glass is heat-treated at 620°C (achieving tempered "safety glass" durability) and paired with a 60A silicone base for a full-footprint grip. **Price: ~$109** (White). Positioned as the accessible / portable entry into the glass lineup.

> Note: WALLHACK also markets a speed-specific 5th-gen surface (**VA-005**) alongside CR-005 as the dual speed/control pairing. The SP-005 carries the legacy "SP" speed lineage.

## What Ships in the Box (5th-gen pads)
5th-generation pads ship as a complete kit: a custom EVA-lined protective carry case, **both PTFE and UHMW-PE silent skates**, and a microfiber maintenance kit — ready to use out of the box.

## Broader Product Range
- **Glass mousepads** — SP-005, CR-005, SP-004A, plus the older SP-004 (core business).
- **Cloth / soft mousepads** — e.g. MO-001, SY-001, for control-focused players.
- **Mouse skates** — universal PTFE and UHMW-PE silent skates for glass surfaces.
- **Pro Sleeve** — arm sleeve for friction-free control.
- **Bundles** — pad + Pro Sleeve + silent skates as a ready-made desk upgrade.
- **Limited editions & collectibles** — themed drops (e.g. Ghost in the Shell '95, Miku OS) and blind boxes.

## Positioning
WALLHACK frames itself as a maker of **surfaces, not accessories** — competition-grade gear that performs as sharp as it looks. Key selling points vs. cloth: glass doesn't wear out or degrade, glide stays consistent over years, it's easy to clean, and it works with any modern sensor. The brand maintains a close, community-driven relationship with its audience.
